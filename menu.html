01 / COFFEE

Classic coffees

Ristretto Espresso

18g ground coffee, 18–27g espresso

How to make it

Pull a short espresso shot from 18g of finely ground coffee. Stop at 18–27g in the cup.

Double Espresso

18g ground coffee, 36g espresso

How to make it

Pull a double shot from 18g of finely ground coffee. Aim for about 36g in the cup.

Lungo Espresso

18g ground coffee, 54g espresso

How to make it

Pull a longer espresso shot from 18g of ground coffee. Stop at about 54g in the cup.

Americano

1 espresso, 120ml hot water

How to make it

Pour hot water into a cup, then add the espresso.

Café au Lait

120ml brewed coffee, 120ml steamed milk

How to make it

Brew the coffee. Add an equal amount of hot, lightly steamed milk.

Black Coffee

15g ground coffee, 240ml hot water

How to make it

Brew with a filter or press, then serve without milk.

02 / COFFEE

Milk-based coffees

Cappuccino

1 espresso, 120ml milk

How to make it

Pull the espresso. Steam the milk with a thick layer of foam and pour over the shot.

Latte

1 espresso, 180ml milk

How to make it

Pull the espresso. Steam the milk with a thin layer of foam and pour it in.

Flat White

1 double espresso, 120ml milk

How to make it

Pull the double espresso. Steam the milk to fine microfoam and pour gently.

Macchiato

1 espresso, 1–2 tbsp milk foam

How to make it

Pull the espresso and spoon a small amount of milk foam on top.

Cortado

1 double espresso, equal volume of warm milk

How to make it

Pull the double espresso and add roughly the same volume of lightly steamed milk.

Mocha

1 espresso, 1 tbsp chocolate syrup, 150ml milk

How to make it

Mix chocolate syrup with espresso. Add steamed milk and stir.

03 / COLD DRINKS

Iced drinks

Iced Coffee

180ml strong brewed coffee, ice

How to make it

Let the coffee cool, then pour it over a glass full of ice.

Iced Latte

1 espresso, 180ml cold milk, ice

How to make it

Fill a glass with ice and milk. Pour the cooled espresso over it.

Iced Mocha

1 espresso, 1 tbsp chocolate syrup, 150ml milk, ice

How to make it

Mix espresso with chocolate. Add cold milk and pour over ice.

Iced Cappuccino

1 espresso, 120ml milk, ice

How to make it

Pour cooled espresso over ice, add cold milk, then top with cold milk foam.

Cold Brew

60g coarse coffee, 600ml cold water

How to make it

Steep coffee in water in the fridge for 12–16 hours. Strain and serve over ice.

04 / FAVORITES

Specialty & flavored drinks

Hot Chocolate

200ml milk, 2 tbsp chocolate syrup

How to make it

Heat the milk without boiling. Whisk in chocolate syrup until smooth.

White Chocolate Mocha

1 espresso, 25g white chocolate, 150ml milk

How to make it

Melt white chocolate into hot espresso. Add steamed milk and stir.

Caramel Latte

1 espresso, 1 tbsp caramel sauce, 180ml milk

How to make it

Stir caramel into espresso. Pour in steamed milk.

Vanilla Latte

1 espresso, 1 tsp vanilla syrup, 180ml milk

How to make it

Stir vanilla syrup into espresso. Add steamed milk.

Hazelnut Mocha

1 espresso, 1 tbsp hazelnut syrup, 1 tsp cocoa, 150ml milk

How to make it

Mix espresso, hazelnut syrup and cocoa. Add steamed milk and stir.

Matcha Latte

2g matcha, 30ml warm water, 180ml milk

How to make it

Whisk matcha with warm water until smooth. Add hot or cold milk.

05 / TEA & MORE

Tea & milk

Teaberry Tea

Tea blend, 240ml hot water

How to make it

Steep the tea blend in hot water according to its package directions, then strain.

Herbal Tea

1 herbal tea bag or 2g dried herbs, 240ml hot water

How to make it

Steep in hot water for 5–7 minutes, then remove the bag or strain.

Steamed Milk

200ml milk

How to make it

Heat and steam the milk until hot and silky. Serve right away.

Frothy Milk

200ml milk

How to make it

Warm the milk, then froth it with a wand or handheld frother.

06 / DESSERT

Coffee & ice cream

Affogato

1 scoop vanilla ice cream, 1 espresso

How to make it

Place ice cream in a chilled cup and pour hot espresso over it.

Iced Coffee Float

200ml iced coffee, 1 scoop vanilla ice cream

How to make it

Pour iced coffee into a tall glass and add the ice cream.

Mocha Affogato

1 scoop chocolate ice cream, 1 espresso

How to make it

Place chocolate ice cream in a cup and pour hot espresso over it.

Espresso Milkshake

1 espresso, 2 scoops vanilla ice cream, 100ml milk

How to make it

Cool the espresso, then blend it with ice cream and milk until smooth.

Caramel Coffee Sundae

2 scoops vanilla ice cream, 1 espresso, 1 tbsp caramel

How to make it

Scoop ice cream into a bowl. Add espresso and drizzle with caramel.

Nutella Affogato

1 scoop vanilla ice cream, 1 espresso, 1 tsp Nutella

How to make it

Add ice cream to a cup. Spoon on Nutella and pour espresso over it.

Cold Brew Float

200ml cold brew, 1 scoop vanilla ice cream

How to make it

Pour cold brew into a chilled glass and float the ice cream on top.
